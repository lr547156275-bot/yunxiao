# Measured versus interpreted

## Directly measured
Flow/round completion, sender TX events, admission/credit state, queue samples, utilization counters, ECN/PFC rows, rate transitions, and control-message counts.

## Run-level derivations

FCT/CCT, queue AUC summaries, actual aggregate admission rate, reconstructed admission budget, goodput ratios, and paired seed percentage differences.

## Interpretation

Batch coordination explains the E2 oversubscription removal; tracking and credit contributions are ablation interpretations. These are ns-3 fixed-path results, not production or dynamic-routing claims. Three seeds do not establish broad statistical stability.
