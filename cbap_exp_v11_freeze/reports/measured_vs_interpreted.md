# Measured versus interpreted

## Measured

Run-level CCT/FCT, queue, utilization, goodput, fairness, rate checkpoints, ECN/PFC and invariant counts come from retained CSV/JSON outputs.

## Interpreted

The scope restriction follows the preregistered thresholds: E2 passes while E1 and E4 recovery do not. No packet or epoch is treated as an independent sample, and no victim or production-network claim is inferred.
